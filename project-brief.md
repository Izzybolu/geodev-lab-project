# My project brief

## The question

Which settlements in Ibadan North-East LGA sit in low-lying areas near watercourses?

## The data I need

- Ward boundaries GRID3 https://data.grid3.org/datasets
- Settlement extents GRID3 - https://data.grid3.org 

- Watercourses OSM through QuickOSM - extracted for my LGA

- Elevation data - Copernicus DEM 30 m - https://portal.opentopography.org/raster?opentopoID=OTSDEM.032021.4326.3
