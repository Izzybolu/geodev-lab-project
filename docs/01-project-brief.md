## 3. Study area

**Ibadan North-East LGA**

Ibadan North-East is one of the most densely populated local government areas (LGAs) in Oyo State, Nigeria, located approximately between latitudes 7°15′ and 7°30′ N and longitudes 3°45′ and 4°00′ E. It's elevation ranges generally between 150m to 210m above sea level, characterised by undulating terrain intersected by natural watercourses. Because of the area's high settlement density and informal urban growth, many residential and commercial structures have encroached upon natural floodplains, making it a critical area for assessing spatial vulnerability to flooding.
